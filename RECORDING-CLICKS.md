# Recording clicks

Open https://impactor.cosminbararu.com/ and refresh once to load the latest version.

## Prepare before recording

1. Select **Storm + heavy cargo**.
2. Click **Forecast with Jev**. Jev selects one of four simulated strategies.
3. In **Dispatch controller**, select **River · fine-tuned**. Leave **Controller enabled** checked. Jev still plans the shift.
4. Click **Run the shift**. Wait for the requests and animation to finish. The result must say **6 River dispatch decisions**. This can take about a minute.
5. Click **Find relationships**. Wait for **Nine factors tested. Results saved and recalled from GBrain.**
6. Scroll back to the 3D world. Keep the completed shift loaded. Do not click Fresh agent yet.

## Record

| Time | Show | Click |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-15 seconds | 3D delivery world, rain, cargo and battery controls | Orbit gently. Introduce the physical-AI background and repeated validation problem. |
| 15-30 seconds | Can I still use this result? | **Change only paint**, then **Change parcel weight**. The counts change from 9 matching to 1 matching and 8 needing retest. |
| 30-43 seconds | The exact changed conditions and procedure branches | **Show why**. Explain the conditions around GBrain experiments and the execution branches around Memorable procedures. |
| 43-53 seconds | The decisions actually applied to the shift | **Dispatch decisions**. Show the River provider, preflight and chosen actions. Jev's plan selection appears alongside. |
| 53-60 seconds | The training run behind the controller | **River training**. Show the checkpoint's nine held-out action-label matches and six decisions applied in the last shift. |
| Optional ending | Memory transfers to a fresh agent | Scroll up and click **Fresh agent** after showing the River results. Wait for the recall confirmation. |

The video script is in VIDEO-SCRIPT.md. Its spoken order can be recorded over these screens or paired with the screen recording afterward. The demo is simulated; the model calls and memory operations are real.

Keep the authenticated River relay and tunnel running. Inference is on River's GPUs. The relay does not run a model on the laptop.
